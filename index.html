02 / About

The short version

A point of view on the front end. A solid foundation underneath. I like working where those two things meet.

I’m Rehan, a full-stack developer focused on the space between a rough idea and a product people actually want to use.

I join early, ask the inconvenient questions, and stay close enough to the code to make the details feel intentional. The goal is always the same: less friction, more clarity, better work.
